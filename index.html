You can watch four TV streams side by side on one screen using TiviMate’s multiview feature. This guide shows you the exact steps to enable and customize multiview on your device.

No extra hardware or complex software is needed — only a compatible device, TiviMate 5.x, and a stable IPTV subscription.

What you need

  • A device that supports TiviMate 5.x (for example, Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Android TV Box, Xiaomi Mi Box S, Apple TV)
  • TiviMate Premium (the multiview feature is available in the paid version of the app)
  • An active IPTV subscription with at least four working streams.   • Reliable internet connection. To reduce buffering, set your buffer/cache to 5,000 ms and use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) to prevent ISP throttling.

Step-by-step multiview setup

  1. Open TiviMate on your device. Make sure you are logged in with your SubTV IP subscription.
  2. Go to the main menu by pressing the back or menu button on your remote.
  3. Select "Settings" > "General".
  4. Look for "Multiview" and turn it ON. If the option is grayed out, you need TiviMate Premium — upgrade inside the app.
  5. Press the home button on your remote to return to the channel list.
  6. Select a channel and start playing a stream.
  7. While the stream is playing, press the down arrow on your remote to open the playback controls.
  8. Select the "Multiview" icon (two overlapping squares). The screen will split into four quadrants and a small box will show your current stream.
  9. To add a second stream: click on an empty quadrant and choose a channel from your list. Repeat for the third and fourth quadrants.
  10. Press the back button to exit the channel selection. All four streams will play simultaneously.
  11. To switch audio between streams, highlight the quadrant you want to hear and press OK. A small speaker icon will appear.
  12. To change the layout (for example 2x2, 1+3, or 1+1+2), press the menu button inside multiview mode and select "Layout".

If a step fails

  • Multiview option missing: Ensure you have TiviMate Premium (paid version). The free version does not include multiview.
  • Streams won’t load: Check your internet speed. A buffer setting of 5,000 ms and Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) helps. Also confirm your SubTV IP subscription is active — you can message them on WhatsApp for support.
  • Audio only from one quadrant: This is normal. Press OK on the quadrant you want to hear.
  • Screen flickering or lag: 4K/60 fps streams require a device like Fire TV Stick 4K Max or a recent Android TV box. Older devices may not handle four streams well.
